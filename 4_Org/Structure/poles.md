# ATOM Research Poles

The ATOM research program is organized around five interconnected poles that structure our work toward building an agent foundation model.

## 1. World Modeling Pole

Developing universally expressive and tractable hierarchical state-space model classes for representing complex, partially observed environments.

## 2. Model Discovery Pole

Developing statistical inference over heterogeneous spaces of world models.

## 3. Decision-Making Pole

Developing information-theoretic planning schemes for action selection to maximize information gain about model architectures, parameters and/or states, while satisfying utility, safety, and reliability constraints.

## 4. Engineering Pole

Integration of world modeling, model discovery, and decision-making efforts, including integrated codebase, scalability, hardware optimization, and stable releases.

## 5. Exploration Pole

Exploring ideas outside our roadmap relevant to ATOM's four aims [i.e. (1) interaction (action, planning), (2) sample-efficient, continual and open-ended learning, (3) generalization and adaptation under distribution shifts, (4) alignment, safety and robustness] which may feedback into the research roadmap.

---

## Pole Organization

Each of the first three poles (World Modeling, Model Discovery, Decision-Making) is organized into three subcategories:
- **Theory**: Fundamental questions, mathematical foundations, theoretical frameworks
- **Methods**: Algorithmic approaches, inference techniques, computational methods
- **Engineering**: Implementation, scalability, hardware

The Engineering Pole focuses primarily on integration and practical implementation across all three research poles.

The Exploration Pole investigates new ideas that may inform or validate our research directions.
